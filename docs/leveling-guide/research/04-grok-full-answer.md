## 2. REPUTATION LADDERS

### How it works [4.10.3]
- **Earning rep:** you gain or lose reputation with a mission giver by completing or failing its contracts. Fail too many and you are fired for 24 hours. Rep unlocks locations, NPCs, missions, shops, gear and ship items ([starcitizen.tools: Reputation](https://starcitizen.tools/Reputation)).
- **The ladders:** the game files hold **37 factions on 8 rank ladders**, shown below with cumulative rep points (4.10.2 data) ([Citadel](https://www.citadelsc.com/en/contracts/reputations)).
- **Career ladders:** some factions also give separate "career" ladders, such as Hauling, Courier, Security, Bounty, "Hired Muscle" and Assassin ([Dystopian Dynasty: Covalex](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/covalex), [Headhunters](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/headhunters)).
- **What each rank unlocks:** the "new contracts" counts below show how many contracts each rank opens. They are counted from game data (4.10.2 data) on each faction's Dystopian Dynasty page. "Unknown" means no source names the specific unlocks.

### New in 5.0: guild ranks [5.0]
- **Guild ranks:** these sit on top of faction rep. Some career contracts need both, and career contracts raise both. Notice Board jobs give neither ([PPRGaming Patch Watch](https://pprgaming.com/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-patch-watch-guild-ranks-starmap-routes-ground-vehicles-freight-elevators/)).
- **Rank names:** the mobiGlas now shows names for each guild's first six ranks ([starzen.space: 13284307 notes](https://www.starzen.space/t/sc-patch-notes/19320?page=27)). Names read from the game files ([Versefiles](https://versefiles.com/reputation)):
  - **Interstellar Transport Guild:** Trainee → Rookie → Junior → Member → Experienced → Senior → Master
  - **Mercenary Guild:** Under Review → Foot Soldier → Low Level Enforcer → Enforcer → Experienced Enforcer → Trusted Enforcer → Lethal Enforcer. Versefiles marks this ladder mapping as "derived".
- **Still unknown:** the rep thresholds for guild ranks, and which other guilds exist.

## 3. ACTIVITIES

**About the earnings figures:** CIG publishes no aUEC/hour figures. Every range below is a community estimate for the 4.10 cycle, and none was re-measured on 4.10.3 specifically ([MMODude](https://www.mmodude.com/farming-guides/star-citizen-auec-farming-guide/)). **5.0 earnings:** unknown for every activity, because only single PTU payouts have been published.

### Hauling
- **What it is:** moving contract-supplied cargo between locations, with no cargo purchase needed [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-transport-contrats/)).
- **Minimum ship and gear:** any ship with a cargo grid, plus a multitool with a tractor head or a ship tractor beam [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-transport-contrats/)).
- **How a new player starts:** open the mobiGlas Contracts → Hauling tab, take a job that fits your hold, call the cargo to the freight elevator, load it and deliver it [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-transport-contrats/)).
- **Earnings:**
  - 100,000–450,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-transport-contrats/)).
  - Route-stacked with high rep: about 0.9–1.5M aUEC/h (4.10.1 figure) ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-how-to-make-auec-fast-alpha-4-10-1)).
- **Reputation:** Covalex, Red Wind Linehaul, Ling Family Hauling, UDM and FTL Courier (Hauling and Courier careers) [4.10.3].
- **5.0 changes** [5.0] ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-release-date-patch-notes-wipe), [Orvix](https://www.orvixgrid.com/next-patch)):
  - Interstellar Transport Guild rank gates higher contracts.
  - Guild Hall career boards.
  - Freight elevators: multi-mission hand-in, excess cargo returned.
  - Hauling markers point to the nearest freight kiosk.
  - The Transport Guild intro mission is required first.

### Trading
- **What it is:** buying commodities cheap and selling them dear at another terminal [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-commerce-marchandises/)).
- **Minimum ship and gear:** any ship with SCU capacity, plus starting capital. No rep is needed [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-commerce-marchandises/)).
- **How a new player starts:** pick a route with a tool such as [UEX Trade Routes](https://uexcorp.space/trade/routes), check stock and price age, then buy at a commodity terminal and sell at the destination [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-commerce-marchandises/)).
- **Earnings:** 100,000–600,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-commerce-marchandises/)). The risk is the highest of any loop because all the stock is bought ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-how-to-make-auec-fast-alpha-4-10-1)).
- **Reputation:** none.
- **5.0 changes:** unknown, apart from the freight-elevator fixes [5.0].

### Mining
- **What it is:** scanning, fracturing and extracting ore, then refining and selling it [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-minage-vaisseau/)).
  - Since 4.10, ore quality (0–1000) is kept and affects price and crafting.
  - Refined ore is worth about 2–3× raw.
- **Minimum ship and gear:**
  - Cheapest: a multitool with a mining head (hand mining) or a ground vehicle [4.10.3] ([scshipfinder](https://scshipfinder.com/guides/how-to-make-money), [Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-minage-vaisseau/)).
  - Ship mining: MISC Prospector, Drake Golem or Argo MOLE. The Prospector costs about 2,000,000 aUEC; verify at the terminal ([MMODude](https://www.mmodude.com/farming-guides/star-citizen-auec-farming-guide/)).
- **How a new player starts:** no rep is needed to mine freely [4.10.3]. Start at Aaron Halo or a Lagrange point, scan for signatures of about 4,000–8,000, then refine at a Lagrange station refinery ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-minage-vaisseau/)).
- **Earnings:** 200,000–900,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-minage-vaisseau/)).
- **Reputation:** Shubin Interstellar (purchase orders) and Recco Battaglia ("Mining & Local Jobs", Nyx) [4.10.3] ([DD](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/shubin-interstellar), [Citadel](https://www.citadelsc.com/en/contracts/reputations)).
- **5.0 changes:** new mineable rock variants on Nyx I [5.0]. CIG has acknowledged problems with mining node values and with some nodes that can't be mined ([Orvix](https://www.orvixgrid.com/next-patch)).

### Salvage
- **What it is:** scraping wreck hulls into RMC and stripping components to sell [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-recuperation-coques/)).
- **Minimum ship and gear:** a multitool with a salvage head on foot, or a salvage ship. The cheap ship is the Salvation; the step-ups are the Vulture and MOTH [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-recuperation-coques/), [scshipfinder](https://scshipfinder.com/guides/how-to-make-money)).
  - The Vulture costs about 2,513,700 aUEC; verify ([MMODude](https://www.mmodude.com/farming-guides/star-citizen-auec-farming-guide/)).
  - 4.10.3 raised salvage-ship buffers ([StarCitizenHelp](https://starcitizenhelp.com/updates)).
- **How a new player starts:** no rep is needed. Take a salvage contract or find a wreck on scan, scrape it, and sell the RMC, for example at the Orison Cloudview Center TDD [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-recuperation-coques/), [Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-how-to-make-auec-fast-alpha-4-10-1)).
- **Earnings:**
  - 150,000–600,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-recuperation-coques/)).
  - Crewed: about 1M+ aUEC/h (4.10.1 figure) ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-how-to-make-auec-fast-alpha-4-10-1)).
- **Reputation:** Adagio Holdings (salvage contracts and orders); Red Wind Linehaul also gives salvage jobs [4.10.3] ([DD Adagio](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/adagio-holdings), [DD Red Wind](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/red-wind-linehaul)).
- **5.0 changes:** Adagio rep is set up on Nyx I [5.0] ([Orvix](https://www.orvixgrid.com/next-patch)).
  - The Gatac Hyūn (mining and salvage) is Committed on the roadmap. Whether it arrives in 4.10.x or 5.0 is unknown ([PPRGaming roadmap](https://pprgaming.com/news/star-citizen-roadmap-roundup-november-18-alpha-5-0-combat-balance-loot-refresh/)).

### Ship combat / bounty

- **Minimum ship and gear:** any armed ship [4.10.3]. Suggested light fighters: Arrow, Gladius, F7C Hornet Mk II, Cutlass Black ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-chasse-primes/)).
- **How a new player starts:** complete the BHG intro contract in the mobiGlas Bounty tab with CrimeStat 0 [4.10.3]. Then buy the 500 aUEC Tracker Training Permit and take VLRT targets ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-chasse-primes/), [starcitizen.tools](https://starcitizen.tools/Bounty_Hunters_Guild)).
- **Earnings:**
  - 150,000–700,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-chasse-primes/)).
  - Wiki base payouts per target: VLRT 19,000 up to ERT 99,000 aUEC (patch not stated; [starcitizen.tools](https://starcitizen.tools/Bounty_Hunters_Guild)).
  - Top PvE: InterSec "Seek and Destroy Maulers (Super)" listed 2,004,000 aUEC in 4.10.3 PTU data ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-vanduul-incursion-guide-alpha-4-10-3)).
- **Reputation:** Bounty Hunters Guild; Hurston, Crusader, microTech and ArcCorp bounty careers; Northrock; InterSec; Foxwell Enforcement [4.10.3].
- **5.0 changes** [5.0]:
  - TTK pass (see heading 1).
  - High Risk bounties give 4,000 rep.
  - Very Hard and Super bounties are fixed.
  - "Assist Ship In Combat" is disabled.
  - New missile-lock UI ([Orvix](https://www.orvixgrid.com/next-patch)).

### FPS / ground combat
- **What it is:** on-foot bunker and outpost clearing, and FPS bounties [4.10.3] ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-missions-bunker/)).

- **Earnings:** 80,000–300,000 aUEC/h [4.10.3] ([Beacon, 4.10](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-missions-bunker/)).

  - Turrets with 50% more health.
  - Climbable ledges.
  - A loot pass.
  - Mercenary Guild rank gate.

## 4. FIRST STEPS (brand-new player, 4.10.3)

All 15 objectives are [4.10.3]. The money figures are the sourced amounts added together.

**1. Pick a Stanton home location.**
- **What:** at character creation, choose New Babbage, Area18, Orison or Lorville. RSI recommends Stanton because Pyro and Nyx are much more dangerous ([RSI KB: Locations](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008085873-Locations-in-Star-Citizen)).
- **Done when:** you wake up in a hab in that city. New accounts start with 20,000 aUEC ([StarCitizenHelp](https://starcitizenhelp.com/updates)).

**2. Complete "Tutorial - Your First Steps".**
- **What:** inside your hab, learn movement and the mobiGlas: the Contract Manager, Journal, hunger and thirst.
- **Where:** your hab. The database entry shows the Orison variant; [sc-codex](https://sc-codex.ru/contracts/tutorial-your-first-flight-527a61ef) lists 4 variants for the last tutorial part.
- **Done when:** the contract completes and **15,000 aUEC** is paid ([star-citizen.wiki](https://api.star-citizen.wiki/missions/tutorial-your-first-steps-4)).

**3. Complete "Tutorial - Your First Outing".**
- **What:** visit the Kel-To weapons store in the city and learn shopping.
- **Done when:** the contract completes and **15,000 aUEC** is paid ([star-citizen.wiki](https://api.star-citizen.wiki/missions/tutorial-your-first-outing-4)).

**4. Complete "Tutorial - Your First Flight".**
- **What:** take transit to the spaceport, fly the Anvil C8 Pisces you are lent, and land. In 4.10.3, landing with autoland now completes the landing objective ([StarCitizenHelp](https://starcitizenhelp.com/updates)).
- **Done when:** the contract completes and **40,000 aUEC** is paid ([sc-codex](https://sc-codex.ru/contracts/tutorial-your-first-flight-527a61ef)).
- **Running total:** about 90,000 aUEC (20,000 + 15,000 + 15,000 + 40,000).

**5. Retrieve your own ship.**
- **What:** at an ASOP terminal in the spaceport, retrieve your pledge ship, power it up, request clearance and launch ([RSI KB: Now Leaving Lorville](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048403313-Now-Leaving-Lorville)).
- **Done when:** the ship is on your pad or in your hangar, and you reach space.

**6. Do a first courier (package) contract for Covalex or Red Wind Linehaul.**
- **Where:** mobiGlas → Contracts.
- **Done when:** the Courier career shows **Jr. Runner**, which needs 1 rep ([DD: Covalex](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/covalex)).

**7. Do a first hauling contract.**
- **What:** load the cargo with a tractor beam and deliver it to a freight elevator ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-transport-contrats/)).
- **Done when:** the Hauling career reaches **Rookie**, which needs 50 rep ([Citadel](https://www.citadelsc.com/en/contracts/reputations)).

**8. Reach Hauling Junior.**
- **What:** keep hauling.
- **Done when:** the reputation page shows **Junior**, which needs 250 rep. This opens Junior Rank contracts ([starcitizen.tools: Covalex](https://starcitizen.tools/Covalex_Shipping)).

**9. Money milestone: 150,000 aUEC.**
- **What:** a target I set; it is not an in-game objective.
- **How:** keep stacking hauling and courier jobs until you cover licenses, gear and a rental.
- **Done when:** the mobiGlas wallet shows 150,000 or more.

**10. Join the Bounty Hunters Guild.**
- **What:** do the BHG intro contract (mobiGlas Bounty tab, CrimeStat 0) ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-chasse-primes/)). Then buy the **Tracker Training Permit** for 500 aUEC ([starcitizen.tools: BHG](https://starcitizen.tools/Bounty_Hunters_Guild)).
- **Done when:** your rank is **Probationary Guild Member** and VLRT bounties appear.

**11. Reach Junior Guild Member.**
- **What:** complete VLRT bounties until you reach 3,000 rep ([Citadel](https://www.citadelsc.com/en/contracts/reputations)). Then pay 1,000 aUEC for the **Tracker Beginner's Permit**.
- **Done when:** LRT bounties appear in the contract list ([starcitizen.tools](https://starcitizen.tools/Bounty_Hunters_Guild)).

**12. Rent a mining (or salvage) ship.**
- **What:** at Vantage Rentals in Teasa Spaceport (Lorville), rent a Prospector for a day. It has a fixed loadout ([RSI KB: Renting](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013172187-Renting-Ships-Equipment), [UEX: Vantage Lorville](https://uexcorp.space/terminals/info/name/vantage-rentals-teasa-spaceport-lorville/tab/rent/)).
- **Done when:** the rented ship appears at the ASOP terminal with a rental timer.

**13. Mine, refine and sell your first load.**
- **What:** start at Aaron Halo or a Lagrange point. Refine at a Lagrange station refinery, then sell ([Beacon](https://beacon-mag.com/en/forum/star-citizen-activites/activite-star-citizen-minage-vaisseau/)).
- **Done when:** the refinery job shows complete and the sale shows on the commodity terminal.

**14. Craft your first item.**
- **What:** buy an Item Fabricator, place it in your hangar, and run a starting blueprint using Autofill or materials you add by hand ([DD: Crafting](https://www.dystopiandynasty.org/guides/crafting)).
- **Done when:** the crafted item appears in your local inventory.

**15. Money milestone: buy your first earning ship.**
- **What:** buy a MISC Prospector (about 2,000,000 aUEC) or a Drake Vulture (about 2,513,700 aUEC). These are community prices; verify them at the dealer ([MMODude](https://www.mmodude.com/farming-guides/star-citizen-auec-farming-guide/)).
- **Where:** for example New Deal in Lorville ([StarCitizenHelp](https://starcitizenhelp.com/updates)).
- **Done when:** the ship is listed as owned at the ASOP terminal, with no rental timer.

### What 5.0 changes in these 15 [5.0]
- **Steps 1–4:** no tutorial or starting-money changes found (unknown).
  - No wipe is planned, so 4.10.3 progress should carry over ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-release-date-patch-notes-wipe)).
  - Starting in Pyro or Nyx is already an option on LIVE ([RSI KB](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008085873-Locations-in-Star-Citizen)). Whether 5.0 adds a Nyx I start is unknown.
