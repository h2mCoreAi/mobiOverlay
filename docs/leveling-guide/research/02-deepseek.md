Here is an ordered leveling guide for Star Citizen, structured like a World of Warcraft quest log. It is based on the current **4.10.3 LIVE** patch and the **5.0 PTU**, with every objective tagged to the correct version.

### Phase 1: Early Game & Foundation [4.10.3]

This phase focuses on building your initial funds and reputation with accessible contracts. The goal is to establish a reliable income stream and unlock your first ship upgrade.

*   **Objective: Reach 50,000 aUEC.** This is an early money milestone. Reliable sources of income include low-risk bounty hunting, small cargo hauls, and hand mining.
*   **Objective: Reach "Rookie" rank (50 rep) with Covalex.** Start with local delivery and hauling contracts. This is the first step on the Covalex reputation ladder.
*   **Objective: Purchase your first ship upgrade: the Drake Pitbull.** This is a versatile and affordable starter ship. It can be purchased for approximately **606,000 aUEC** at locations like "Buy and Fly" in Bloom or Terminus.
*   **Objective: Master Basic Piloting Skills.** Practice using your thrusters, keeping your throttle in the "blue" zone for optimal turning, and maintaining situational awareness in combat.

### Phase 2: Mid-Game & Specialization [4.10.3]

With a better ship and some savings, you can now pursue more lucrative and specialized career paths.

*   **Objective: Reach "Junior Contractor" (800 rep) with InterSec.** This unlocks the **"Shattered Blade Raid"** mission, which pays **72,500 aUEC** and rewards the blueprint for the new **LBCO Skarnov Rifle**.
*   **Objective: Accumulate 1.5 million aUEC.** This milestone opens up a significant upgrade path. A great all-rounder is the **Aegis Avenger Titan**, priced around **1.29 million aUEC**.
*   **Objective: Reach "Member" rank (5,250 rep) with Covalex.** This unlocks more valuable "Member Rank" cargo hauling contracts, such as a "Direct Medium Cargo Haul" that pays over **101,000 aUEC**.
*   **Objective: Reach "Contractor" (2,200 rep) with InterSec.** This grants access to the **"Retake Boarded Vessel"** contract, a key step in the Vanduul Incursion storyline.

### Phase 3: High-End PvE & Big Money [4.10.3]

This phase is for veteran players looking to tackle the most challenging content and earn top-tier rewards.

*   **Objective: Reach "Veteran Contractor" (15,000 rep) with InterSec.** This is a major milestone. It unlocks the most substantial **Vanduul Incursion** contracts, including the "Vanduul Clan Extermination" line, which can pay up to **493,250 aUEC**.
*   **Objective: Earn 8 million aUEC and buy the Origin M80.** This heavy fighter is considered the best firepower-per-aUEC ship in the patch, available for just under **8 million aUEC** at Astro Armada in Area18.
*   **Objective: Reach "Senior Contractor" (95,250 rep max track) with InterSec.** At this rank, you unlock the **"Seek and Destroy Maulers"** missions. The "Super" tier of this mission pays a massive **2,004,000 aUEC** and drops blueprints for the Behring M3A/M4A/M5A cannons. Note that the toughest tiers are designed for crews.
*   **Objective: Acquire the Drake Golem (via Recco Battaglia).** Reaching **Prestige 1 (10,800 rep)** with Recco Battaglia unlocks the "Extra Special Job" mission, which rewards the Drake Golem Alliance ship.

### Phase 4: The Future - Alpha 5.0 PTU

**Disclaimer:** All 5.0 content is on the PTU and is subject to change. Progression is accelerated (with a **25x-50x reputation multiplier**) for testing purposes.

*   **Objective [5.0]: Explore Nyx I and its Guild Halls.** The planet Nyx I has received a major environment pass. Guild Halls with mission boards are now present, introducing a new layer of **guild reputation** on top of existing faction rep.
*   **Objective [5.0]: Test the new Guild Reputation system.** Guilds like the Mercenary, Industrial, and Science guilds have their own reputation tracks. Certain high-level contracts will require you to have a high enough rank in the relevant guild.
*   **Objective [5.0]: Try out new ships for sale on the PTU.** Several new ships are available for purchase in-game, including the **Ironclad Assault, Pitbull, Starlight, Tiburon, and M80**. Their prices are still subject to change.
*   **Objective [5.0]: Refine your combat skills.** The PTU features a **Time-to-Kill (TTK) ship balance pass** and a new **Missile Lock Interface**. Use these changes to adapt and perfect your dogfighting techniques.
