## 5.0 reputation changes (sourced to RSI posts)

- 50x PTU reputation multiplier (testing only).
  [RSI 5.0 PTU notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6-1/9262668)
- Guild reputation pages show names and icons for the first six ranks.
  (same source)
- Contract Boards in Guild Halls and hubs: local, regional, global and system
  missions with faction filters.
  [starcitizen.tools 5.0](https://starcitizen.tools/Update:Star_Citizen_Alpha_5.0.0)
- Some career contracts need both faction standing and guild rank; completing
  them raises both.
  [RSI 5.0 Patch Watch](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/alpha-5-0-patch-watch-new-horizons-new-opportuniti/9264785)
- Higher-ranked hauling contracts cover longer distances and pay more;
  experienced haulers can still take lower-ranked contracts. (Patch Watch)
- Bounty target names highlighted in bounty descriptions. (PTU notes)
